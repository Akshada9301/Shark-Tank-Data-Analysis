
# Shark Tank India SQL Case Study

## Project Overview

Watching Shark Tank India is inspiring, but analyzing its data reveals even deeper business insights. In this project, I used SQL to solve real-world business problems based on the Shark Tank India dataset.

The objective was to transform raw startup and investment data into meaningful insights that support business and investment decisions.

---

## 📊 Business Problems Solved

1. Identified the highest-funded startup in each industry.
2. Found industries with a Female-to-Male pitcher ratio greater than 70%.
3. Analyzed season-wise pitch, offer, and deal conversion rates.
4. Identified the highest-performing season and top 5 industries based on average monthly sales.
5. Analyzed industries with consistent funding growth across seasons.
6. Compared Sharks based on their average investment per deal.
7. Developed a stored procedure to analyze season-wise, shark-wise investment distribution.
8. Identified the Shark with the most diversified investment portfolio.
9. Explained the concept of MySQL indexes and their impact on query performance.
